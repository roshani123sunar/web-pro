How to Make Macaroni

What You Need

  • 200g macaroni
  • 1 liter water
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1/2 cup grated cheese

Steps

  1. Fill a pot with water and add salt.
  2. Bring the water to a boil and add the macaroni — cook for 8 minutes until tender.
  3. Stir occasionally to prevent the macaroni from sticking together.
  4. Drain the macaroni and return it to the pot.
  5. Add butter and cheese, then mix well. For extra flavor, add a little black pepper before serving.
